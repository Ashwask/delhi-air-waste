Delhi Air + Waste: A Systemic Landscape

One interlocked system, not two problems. Built for the RF + RM task force to see the crisis, the leverage, and the partners in one place. All figures are sourced and honesty-flagged where contested. Draft v0.1, 13 Sep 2026.

The crisis in numbers

Delhi's air is improving off a catastrophic base but is still the worst among world capitals; its waste system dumps a city's worth of garbage into landfills every single day. The two are linked.

Air quality, last 8 years (Jan to Nov average)

PM2.5 in ug/m3. Government CAQM/PIB Jan-Nov series (excludes December, the worst month). National annual standard = 40; WHO = 5.

Where the pollution comes from

Within Delhi city, transport leads; across the wider NCR, residential + industry + farm-burning dominate. ~65 to 70% of winter PM2.5 is regional (Haryana/Punjab/UP). Sources: TERI-ARAI (2018) and the government's unified source-apportionment report, 2024 (summary).

Generated vs processed (TPD)

        If we set up a task force, how

        Who is in the room: conveners (RF + RM), a government owner (MCD / DPCC / CAQM), 2 to 3 implementing partners per lever (from the directory below), a funder / CSR, a data and research anchor, and community and advocacy voices.
        How it runs: a small steering group plus one working group per lever, meeting monthly, with this dashboard as the shared tracker. Pilot first, then scale what works.

        What we can leverage (already exists)

        Systems and work already running that a task force can plug into, rather than build from scratch.

        Most of what is needed already exists or is already paid for. The job is to redirect idle money and switch on assets that are built but unused, not to build new things.
