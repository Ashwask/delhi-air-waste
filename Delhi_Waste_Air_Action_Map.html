Delhi Waste + Air: Action & Partner Map

One-look mapping for the RF + RM task force · Draft v1 · 13 Sep 2026

The point of this page: for every stream, what is broken, the quick win we can chase in under a year, and who can actually do it. Detail and sources live in the two companion files. This is the decision sheet.

The picture in 5 lines

  1. Delhi makes ~11,850 TPD of solid waste and processes only ~64%: a ~4,200 TPD gap dumped every day.
  2. The real problem is not disposal, it is segregation (~55%): fix the front end and half the streams improve at once.
  3. Air and waste are one crisis: open burning, landfill fires/methane, and C&D dust are direct PM sources.
  4. ~65 to 70% of winter PM2.5 is regional (Haryana/Punjab/UP): a given, not our project.
  5. So the task force should chase in-our-control + quick + has-a-willing-owner. Everything below is filtered for that.

C. START HERE: the 5 do-first moves

Ranked by in-our-control x impact x a partner who wants it.

  1. Bulk-generator + RWA segregation and composting (wet + dry). Biggest single lever, visible, high buy-in. → MCD + Daily Dump / Saahas / GPS Renewables.
  2. Formalise the informal recyclers (dry + plastic + e-waste). Cheap, proven, creates jobs, diverts 15 to 20% already. → Chintan / Safai Sena + MCD.
  3. Seelampur e-waste safe-dismantling pilot. Clear air + health win in a defined geography. → Toxics Link + a formal recycler.
  4. C&D dust enforcement + recycled-product tender mandate. Directly cuts winter dust; GRAP-aligned; govt controls the offtake. → MCD + PWD / CPWD.
  5. Monitoring-integrity fix + sensor mesh. Makes every other action measurable and accountable. → CPCB / CSTEP + sensor firms.

D. What we will NOT try to fix (the givens: manage, do not chase)

  • Winter meteorology / boundary-layer collapse (physics of the season).
  • The regional airshed (~65 to 70% of winter PM2.5): needs CAQM inter-state action, not a Delhi pilot.
  • The legacy landfill stock: a multi-year drawdown, already underway.
