# Delhi Air + Waste: A Systemic Landscape

An offline, single-file dashboard that treats Delhi's air pollution and municipal solid waste as **one interlocked system**, and turns the evidence into an owned, pre-winter action plan for the NCR airshed. It maps the crisis, the leverage, the regulatory sandbox that lets five states act without new law, the twelve actions with owners and budgets, and the organisations and hardware behind each one.

**Live:** https://ashwask.github.io/delhi-air-waste/ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Draft v0.7

## Tabs

1. **The Crisis** - the numbers and 5-year trend, source apportionment (with the stubble satellite-count artifact and the secondary-aerosol chemistry flagged), the airshed city by city (Delhi 97 down to Meerut 63), the waste ledger, a Delhi hotspot map on the real NCT boundary, and **"the hardware, and who owns it"**: the coal plants ringing the airshed by owner, the four Delhi waste-to-energy operators, the recovery-unit gap, and the reference vs low-cost monitor layer. Ends with the measures in place, who governs what, and the deadlines that are slipping.

2. **The System** - the air-waste chain as one loop, working vs not working (with numbers), the four front-end fixes ranked by impact, the household-fuel lever, and **the six regional programmes** (each a rule to suspend and a number to move, tested through the sandbox), plus the financials of what can be redirected and the Beijing / World Bank cost curve.

3. **Action & Partners** - **the twelve actions before winter ("the action government")**: each with its deadline, government owner, order-of-magnitude budget, the innovator/research/grassroots/think-tank role, and the Upwind evidence it rests on. Followed by a targets-and-measures table, the regulatory sandbox and upwind-first sequencing, the public one-page district artefact, a stream-by-stream action map, the existing assets to leverage (including the `oaq.notf.in` open-data broker), and an ecosystem directory of organisations.

## The frame

One regional **sandbox** signed by the five airshed states and the Commission: a new instrument runs under public before-and-after measurement for a fixed term, the process rule it suspends is named, and what moves the number becomes the rule. Nothing in year one needs a new appropriation; the money is idle, not absent.

## Notes

- Every figure is sourced; contested or unverifiable figures are flagged in place (e.g. the five-state MRF "1,200 on paper" count could not be traced to a primary source and is marked as such).
- Evidence traces to the companion data story **Upwind** (https://upwind.surge.sh) and to **The Action Government** brief.
- This is a **public draft**, built to iterate. Nothing here is a committed plan.
- Self-contained: one `index.html`, no CDN, no keys, works offline.
